## Failure analysis and limits

An initial two-candidate grid (LR 0.01/0.05, decay 0.01) gave 22% checkpoint, 28% random, and 20% pixel validation accuracy. Pixels achieved 100% training accuracy with validation cross-entropy 102.4547, exposing severe overfitting/overconfidence. The shared grid was then expanded for all representations. Both complete reports remain local; this adaptation used validation, so the final scores are exploratory selection outcomes.
Pixels still fit every training example and have high validation loss. The random encoder scored higher than the early learned checkpoint on this subset. Three examples separate their validation counts; one small seed and reused validation cannot establish a reliable ranking. The encoder has only 23 updates, and no sustained-training or pretrained comparison was run.
