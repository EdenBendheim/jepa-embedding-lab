## What ran

Compared fixed 2D sin/cos positions at multipliers **0.02 and 1.0**, with
identical non-scale architecture and 29,472 trainable parameters. Three encoder
seeds, 2,223 updates each, batch four, width 32, four heads and encoder/predictor
depths 2/1. Trained the three .02 models locally on one CPU thread; reused the
three pinned October 18 scale-one checkpoints. Complete journals reproduce
identical actual image order within each seed: 8,892 draws and 8,061–8,112 unique
training images. Equal updates/capacity are controlled, not a wall-clock claim.

Selected AdamW/ridge heads and neighbor counts on the historical 500 fitting /
200 tuning rows using the predeclared grids and seeds. Each scale has its exact
initialization random control; pixels are shared and duplicate pixel states
must reproduce identically. Saved states and all reported neighbor candidates
were audited, then every selection was locked **before** fresh extraction.

Scored the originally reserved 500 balanced validation rows, excluding all
2,246 declared earlier observations. No fresh-row normalization fitting,
classifier fitting or setting selection occurred. Before access, a source-pinned
exposure extension and lock-bound receipt conservatively consume the reservation;
interrupted attempts retain it. The complete ledger now covers **2,746** observed
validation rows, leaving **2,254** (225–227/class). Original history files and
bundle remain unchanged. The official test set remains reserved.

## Fresh validation accuracy

AdamW averages three classifier seeds within each of three encoder seeds.
Ridge/kNN average encoder seeds. Pixels use one shared representation. All
results use the same 500 examples; seed summaries are descriptive variation,
not independent dataset samples or confidence intervals.

| Representation | AdamW | Ridge | Cosine kNN |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Trained, scale .02 | 25.53% | 25.00% | 22.73% |
| Random, scale .02 | 25.38% | 25.53% | 20.67% |
| Trained, scale 1.0 | 26.51% | 27.67% | 20.40% |
| Random, scale 1.0 | 26.60% | 25.53% | 19.20% |
| Pixels | 24.60% | 27.20% | 25.60% |

The predeclared primary contrast, **trained .02 minus 1.0 AdamW**, is **−0.98
percentage points**. Across the nine matched head comparisons, 545 predictions
were recovered and 589 regressed; the same 500 examples appear nine times, so
these are not 4,500 independent examples. Ridge gives −2.67 points, with 148
recoveries and 188 regressions across three paired runs. Smaller amplitude
improves this kNN comparison by 2.33 points, but remains below pixel kNN.

Trained .02 AdamW is only +0.16 points above its matched random control;
trained 1.0 is −0.09 points below its random control. A broad learned-feature
advantage is **not established**, and reducing the positional signal did not
improve the primary comparison. Do not select a favorable secondary metric or
seed as the experiment's conclusion.

Encoder-seed AdamW means are 25.33%, 25.80%, 25.47% at .02 versus 28.40%,
26.07%, 25.07% at 1.0. One seed favors the smaller scale. These rows differ
from October 18's evaluation, so changes in absolute scores across dates are
not model improvement/regression. This compact, single-target-block experiment
is not a reproduction of the full I-JEPA recipe.

## Reproducibility and privacy

- Reproduced **60 linear artifacts (52 unique states)** and **13 neighbor
  indices**, including candidate grids, original tuning results, selected state
  identity and paired fresh errors. Equal capacity and actual image order are
  verified from the source-bound journals.
- A real cache-only replay with training, raw-image access, extraction and
  classifier/neighbor fitting disabled reproduced the summary byte for byte.
  All 175 local tests, mask demo and three-step synthetic training smoke passed.
- Synthetic checks cover lock/receipt ordering, interrupted recovery, changed
  sources/journals/features/receipts, complete neighbor-candidate replay and
  preservation of previous reports on failure. The full suite also runs in CI.
- Public files contain aggregate counts, class confusion matrices, recipes and
  hashes. Images, feature vectors, model/classifier/index artifacts, exact
  row selections, source bundles and per-image training journals remain ignored.
  No private messages, research data, paid APIs or cloud compute were used.
