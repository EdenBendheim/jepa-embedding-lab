## What the pilot tells us

Adding spatial variance improved this learned encoder's AdamW mean by 2
percentage points, with paired gains of 5/4/3 correct predictions on the
same 200 images across the three classifier seeds. However, its matching
random encoder scored higher, by 5.17 points. The changed readout and doubled
classifier dimension are sufficient alternative explanations for the gain;
these results **do not establish a benefit from JEPA training**.

Max pooling left the learned AdamW mean unchanged and lowered its selected
ridge score by 3 points. No pooling rule is selected for a final test run.
The existing mean scores and validation data had already been used for
tuning. All scores are validation-selected, on one fixed learned and one
random encoder, and neither significance nor independent generalization is claimed.

## Geometry and reproducibility

Learned mean features have numerical rank 31 but covariance entropy effective
rank about 2.10, versus 3.13 for the matching random features. Mean+std raises
these to 63 and 2.62 (random: 63 and 3.84). Variation is concentrated in a few
directions; this is a descriptive finding, not proof of collapse or poor
generalization. Mean+std's extra dimensions and very different pixel input
dimension limit direct cross-representation rank comparisons.

All **28 selected classifiers** (21 AdamW and seven ridge) were reloaded with
exact source identity and reproduced both training/validation scores and
paired errors without refitting. Pixel artifacts/classifiers were shared
across pooling arms. The full synthetic suite passed **77 tests**. No official
test score, private/research data, images, features or model states are published;
the source/selected-state fingerprints detect inconsistency, not authenticity.
