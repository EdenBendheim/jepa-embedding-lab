The 4x4 learned mean gained 0.83 percentage points over this default
trajectory, but remained below pixels. Paired changes were 19 recoveries /
16 regressions, 13/13 and 15/13 across the AdamW seeds; ridge had 9/8.
These are descriptive counts on reused, validation-selected data; no
significance or independent generalization is claimed. This remains one
encoder-training seed, and favorable relative-to-random results do not
establish a reliable useful-training advantage.

## What changed in the workload

Target tokens increased **4→16**, visible context tokens fell **45→36**,
and unused tokens fell **15→12**. The complete partitions are
default 45+4+15=64 and custom 36+16+12=64.
Both runs had 223 optimizer steps with batch four; their attention/prediction
work and information budgets differ. This cannot isolate geometric shape
from target coverage or compute. All masks use a 0.75 fraction of non-target
patches, with a seeded rectangular target and context sampled outside it.

Custom training loss was finite, from 0.5149 at the first recorded step to
0.2468 at the last. Different target sets limit interpretation of loss
comparisons, and lower latent loss alone is not a quality result.
Mean feature numerical rank stayed 31; covariance entropy effective rank
was 2.39 versus 2.10 for the default. No collapse guarantee is inferred.

## Verification and next work

All **12 selected custom-run classifiers** reproduce their training/validation
scores and paired errors. The 28 phase-A states were also rechecked as the
comparison anchor. Actual image order matched both the historical logs and
the deterministic sampler; the public report includes its fingerprint.
The cached training log is bound to exact checkpoint bytes: a simulated
digest mismatch was rejected before extraction, preserving the prior report.
Repeating with the training function disabled reused the checkpoint and
produced a byte-identical public summary. All raw inputs, features, weights
and logs remain ignored. The synthetic suite has 77 passing tests.

Next: choose and predeclare a frozen pretrained comparator, strengthen the
training budget and repeat selected mask settings across encoder seeds.
Use fresh confirmation examples before final protocol selection; keep the
official test set reserved. Width/input-feature studies, plots and an
embedding explorer remain unfinished.
