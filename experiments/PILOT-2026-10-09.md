## Result

500 training / 200 validation examples, balanced within fixed partitions; one 223-step JEPA checkpoint, one matching random encoder, and scaled raw pixels. AdamW candidates receive identical 200-step budgets and grids; the three seeds vary only classifier initialization. Ridge uses an identical penalty grid for each representation and is deterministic.

Standard deviations are in percentage points across classifier initialization. They are not confidence intervals, independent encoder-training runs, or data-sampling uncertainty. The same validation examples select hyperparameters and produce these scores. Ridge scores are one-hot least-squares outputs; its MSE/cross-entropy diagnostics are not calibrated probabilities.

The checkpoint's AdamW mean is slightly above the random encoder here, but the difference is too small and the experiment too limited to establish useful learned-feature improvement. The pixel baseline remains ahead under both heads. The original 23-step checkpoint was evaluated on different examples/grids October 7, so those scores are not a controlled before/after comparison with this run.

## Training and checks

- Resumed the original checkpoint for 200 additional batch-four steps, reaching 223 total. Every sampled image belonged to the fixed training partition; labels were not part of the JEPA objective. The original checkpoint was retained.
- Loss at step 24 was 0.277407 and at step 223 was 0.107335. Target embedding mean standard deviation was 0.669266 then 0.655759; finite nonzero variance is a diagnostic, not proof of representation quality or immunity to collapse.
- Nine selected AdamW classifiers and three selected ridge classifiers were saved and reloaded. All twelve reproduced their exact recorded validation scores on bound verified features. The standalone scoring CLI also reproduced one checkpoint classifier without refitting.
- All 54 local unit/integration tests passed, including source identity, atomic replacement, no test sampling, frozen inputs, numerical validation, primal/dual ridge equivalence, seed fairness and restored scores.
- No official test scoring, paid compute/API, private messages/research data, or public image/feature/weight artifacts.

## Reproduce the run locally

Use the version-one data manifest and a 23-step checkpoint created by the documented training path in the same recorded CPU/PyTorch environment. The checkpoint hash records exact local bytes, not portable serialization identity.

```sh
export PYTHONPATH=src
.venv/bin/python -m jepa_lab.selection data/cifar-10-batches-bin \
  --manifest runs/cifar10-manifest.json --seed 20261009 \
  --train-per-class 50 --validation-per-class 20 --output runs/pilot-selection-20261009.json
.venv/bin/python -m jepa_lab.cifar10_train data/cifar-10-batches-bin \
  --manifest runs/cifar10-manifest.json --steps 200 \
  --resume checkpoints/cifar10-pilot.pt --checkpoint checkpoints/cifar10-pilot-20261009.pt \
  --output runs/cifar10-training-20261009.json
.venv/bin/python -m jepa_lab.compare data/cifar-10-batches-bin \
  --manifest runs/cifar10-manifest.json --selection runs/pilot-selection-20261009.json \
  --checkpoint checkpoints/cifar10-pilot-20261009.pt \
  --feature-dir runs/pilot-features-20261009 --probe-dir runs/pilot-probes-20261009 \
  --steps 200 --learning-rates 0.0001 0.001 --weight-decays 0.1 1 \
  --probe-seeds 29 31 37 --random-seed 29 --ridge-alphas 0.01 0.1 1 10 \
  --output runs/pilot-comparison-20261009.json
```

Use each recorded classifier path with `jepa_lab.probe_score` and its matching feature files to reproduce scores without fitting. Reproduction across hardware/library versions is not guaranteed; source/runtime mismatches are rejected where recorded.
